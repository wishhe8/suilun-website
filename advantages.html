Factory Advantages

A professional dual-base door hinge manufacturer with standardized production facilities in China and Thailand — 30+ years supplying U.S. door factories, fully certified to American standards.

Our group has more than 30 years of dedicated experience supplying door hinges to United States door manufacturers. We are highly specialized in matching North American residential door manufacturing standards, assembly requirements, finishing specifications and mass-production stability.

Residential door hinges account for 25%–30% of our total production volume, while we fully cover commercial Grade 1 heavy-duty hinges and UL fire-rated hinge series — supporting full product-line procurement for large U.S. door factories. Monthly capacity reaches 10 million hinges, with complete stamping, polishing, assembly and packaging lines plus 8 electroplating / electrophoresis lines.

With complete American-standard certifications, global compliance qualifications, and a unified digital ERP production management system, we are fully capable of undertaking large-volume, long-term framework orders for U.S. residential doors, commercial doors and fire-door projects.
